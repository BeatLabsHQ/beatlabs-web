# Provenance of rasters in public/apps

All files are real brand assets; none is generated.

- nibango-icon.png, curb-icon.png — the products' App Store / Play Store icons, supplied by the owner (BeatLabs FZE LLC).
- raved-icon.png — the new Raved app icon (the "R." mark), pasted by the owner in the design session on 2026-09-02; replaces the earlier raved-icon.jpg.
- raved-screen-1.png, raved-screen-2.png, raved-screen-3.png — real Raved app captures supplied by the owner.
- curb-screen-1.png, curb-screen-2.png, curb-screen-3.png — captures of the real Curb iOS app (Debug build, iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator on iOS 26.2, 1320×2868) taken on 2026-09-03 with the app's screenshot mode and seeded demo subscriptions; the UI is the shipped app, the subscriptions are demo data.
- nibango-screen-1.png, nibango-screen-2.png, nibango-screen-3.png — captures of the real Nibango iOS app (Debug build, iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator on iOS 26.2, 1320×2868) taken on 2026-09-03 with the app's screenshot mode and seeded demo listings (royalty-free Unsplash product photos); the UI is the shipped app, the listings and chat are demo data.
- blab-icon.png — the BLAB app icon, pasted by the owner in the design session on 2026-09-02.
- blab-screen-1.png, blab-screen-2.png, blab-screen-3.png — captures of the real BLAB iOS app (iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator on iOS 26.2, 1320×2868) taken on 2026-09-03 from the English UI with the app’s `-demoUser` demo profile; copied from the owner’s App Store screenshot set (Today, Answer, Worlds). The UI is the shipped app, the posts are demo data.
- truelove-icon.png — the TrueLoveCreative apple-touch-icon fetched from https://truelovecreative.es/apple-icon.png (owner's studio).
- estrela-mark.png — the Estrela Photography signature fetched from https://estrela.photo/img/signature.webp (owner's studio), converted to PNG.
- founder.webp — portrait of Javier Estrela fetched from https://estrela.photo/img/contact-800.webp (owner's own site).
